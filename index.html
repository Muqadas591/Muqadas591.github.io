Deep Dive

Case Study: Banking AI Assistant

RAG Architecture Enterprise Deployment The Bank of Punjab Jun 2026 – Present
The Problem
Banking staff at BOP routinely spent significant time searching internal documentation — policy manuals, compliance guidelines, product FAQs — to answer routine queries. This was slow, inconsistent, and dependent on individual knowledge. The bank needed an intelligent knowledge layer that could respond accurately, cite sources, and stay within compliance boundaries.
My Solution
I designed and implemented an enterprise RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline that combines semantic document retrieval with a large language model for grounded, citation-aware responses. The system is secure, auditable, and handles the ambiguity of natural-language banking queries.
User Query │ ▼ [Query Embedding] ──► Hugging Face Embedding Model │ ▼ [Vector Search] ──► FAISS Index (chunked banking docs) │ ▼ [Context Assembly] ──► Top-K relevant document chunks │ ▼ [LLM Inference] ──► Prompt Engineering + LLM │ ▼ [Response + Sources] ──► User (with citations)
Results & Business Impact
The system significantly reduced the time bank staff spent on internal knowledge queries, improved response consistency, and created an auditable trail of AI-generated answers — critical for regulated financial environments.
