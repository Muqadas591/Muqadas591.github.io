Project Deep Dive

Inside ExceptionOps AI

Invoice AutomationHuman ReviewSynthetic Demo Data
The Business Problem
Invoice processing can involve repetitive document handling, field extraction, comparisons against business records, and manual investigation of mismatches. Reliable workflows also need to handle invalid inputs, duplicate requests, failures, and traceability.
The Application Workflow
ExceptionOps AI organizes invoice handling into a traceable workflow. Extracted information is validated, potential exceptions are surfaced for review, and controlled actions are recorded with an audit trail. Human review remains part of the decision process.
Invoice Document │ ▼ Document Ingestion & Local Storage │ ▼ Extraction → Business Validation │ ▼ Exception Classification & Routing │ ▼ Human Review & Approval │ ▼ Controlled / Simulated Action │ ▼ Audit Trail & Workflow History
